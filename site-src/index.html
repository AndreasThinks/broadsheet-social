Cross-poster

Read and post across Bluesky and Mastodon from one app. It runs entirely on your device: your accounts and posts never pass through anyone else's server.

Staying up to date

The desktop apps update themselves: they fetch new versions in the background and switch over the next time you open them. On Android, install the new APK over the old one, or add this page to Obtainium to get updates automatically.

Notifications

On Android, the app checks for replies, mentions, quotes and follows about every 15 minutes, even when it's closed. Android may check less often to save battery.
